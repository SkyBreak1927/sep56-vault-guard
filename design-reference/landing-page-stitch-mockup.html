Risk Vector Analysis

Why Vault Conformance Matters

Tokenized yield vaults represent the single largest target for mathematical precision drift, rounding arbitrage, and capital dilution exploits across modern decentralized finance.

trending_up

First-Depositor & Inflation Attacks

Malicious actors can artificially inflate share values in empty vaults by donating underlying assets, causing subsequent users' deposits to round down to zero shares and forfeit their funds.

SEP-56 virtual offset mitigations & dead shares validation.
swap_horiz

Rounding Direction Vulnerabilities

Vault contracts that round in favor of the depositor rather than the vault enable arbitrageurs to drain funds incrementally through high-frequency micro-transactions.

Checks deposit/mint (round up) vs. redeem/withdraw (round down).
shield

Cross-Contract Auth & Invariant Drift

Unlike EVM vaults, Soroban requires explicit authorization trees. Missing require_auth boundaries can allow unauthorized asset liquidations.

Validates Address::require_auth caller scoping under all execution paths.
